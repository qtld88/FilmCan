# Demo with made-up cards

**Date:** 2026-11-11 · **Lot:** 8 · **Branch:** `qt1/demo-mode`

## Goal

Someone who has just installed FilmCan has no card and no spare drive at hand. Let them see a real, verified copy in one click, and leave no trace afterwards.

## Expected behaviour

- **Start:** on the empty screen (no movie, or no movie selected), under the "create a movie" button: **Try with made-up cards**, with two lines saying what it does. Not shown while a demo exists.
- **What it creates:** a folder `~/Library/Application Support/FilmCan/Demo` holding two made-up cards (`Demo card A001`: five clips; `Demo sound S001`: three sound files) and two empty made-up drives (`Demo drive 1`, `Demo drive 2`); and a movie named "Demo" with these as sources (the sound card tagged Sound) and destinations, full check on. About 66 MB of made-up media; the clips are random data under camera-like names and are not playable.
- **The copy is real:** the user presses the normal backup button. Same engine, same check, same progress, same history and catalog entries as any movie.
- **A band above the demo movie** says the cards and drives are made up and that the copy is real, with **Stop the demo**. The button is greyed while a backup runs.
- **Stop the demo** removes the demo movie, the folder, the movie's history entries, its catalog rolls and its roll names, and selects another movie if there is one. Demo runs are taken back out of the lifetime backup count.
- **Nothing leaves the Mac:** phone (ntfy) and webhook messages are off for the demo movie. The Mac's own notification still shows.

## Edge cases

- **Quit during the demo:** the Demo movie and its band are still there at the next launch; Stop still cleans up.
- **Demo movie deleted by hand:** the button is offered again; starting a demo first removes what the earlier one left (folder, history, catalog rolls).
- **Not enough room or no write access** for the folder: an alert, nothing half-created is left.
- **Stop with no demo:** does nothing.

## What does not change

- The copy engine, verification, manifests. No stored field is added: the demo movie is an ordinary movie, recognised by its id kept in the preferences (`demoConfigId`).
- Any other movie, its history, catalog rolls and roll names (test).
- The debug-only screenshot mode (`Sources/Debug/DemoMode.swift`) is untouched and unrelated.

## Decisions

- Folders, not mounted disk images: no external tool, nothing to unmount, nothing that can stay mounted after a crash. Cost: the made-up cards are not "drives" (no capacity bar of their own, no eject, no card recognition).
- Demo runs do appear in History and the Catalog while the demo exists, tied to the demo movie only, and are removed with it. Hiding them would hide two things worth showing.

## Success criteria

- One click creates the demo; the made-up cards go through the real engine with the full check to both drives, byte for byte (test).
- Stop removes everything of the demo and nothing else: movie, folder, history, catalog rolls, roll names; saved (test).
- Phone and webhook messages are off for the demo (test on the rule).

## Not in this lot

- Linking the demo to the quick tour.
- Made-up cards that are recognised as camera cards, or that play as video.
